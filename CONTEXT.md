# Project context

Status: vocabulary from supplied brief; reviewed 2026-11-14.

**Group**: A collection of members sharing expenses.

**Expense**: A cost paid by one member and allocated among group members.

**Share**: A member's allocated portion of an expense.

**Balance**: A member's net position; a positive value means money is owed to that member.
